Redesigning Ziba de Beauté

Ziba Ahmadi runs two threading salons in Gainesville. The old WordPress site had the right information spread across five pages, with template leftovers, typos and out-of-date details. The new site puts the full menu, prices, deal days, both locations and proof of reputation on one page, and links each service straight to its booking page.

Every finding below was checked against the live old site on October 12, 2026. Scores marked "pending" are filled in by scripts/measure.mjs, which runs Google PageSpeed Insights on both sites; nothing on this page is estimated.
