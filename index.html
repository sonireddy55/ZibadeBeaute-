Meet Ziba

Ziba is a World Record Threader. Through her many years of experience she has become widely known across Florida as the best. She keeps providing her service to customers across the state and keeps branching out to new places where she is needed, and her hard work has earned her a loyal following.

She shapes the arch with the thread itself, not tweezers, and keeps brows full and natural.
